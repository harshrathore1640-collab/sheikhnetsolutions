Our Core Offerings

Professional Home Safety & Neting Services

Engineered for durability, aesthetic elegance, and absolute peace of mind.

Invisible Grills

High-tensile Grade 316 stainless steel wire invisible grills for balconies and windows. Provides unbreakable child safety without obstructing outdoor views or natural breeze.

Balcony Safety Nets

Heavy-duty nylon safety netting designed specifically for high-rise apartment balconies. Protects children and pets from accidental falls securely and discreetly.

Mosquito Nets for Windows & Doors

Fiberglass and stainless steel mesh mosquito nets with Velcro, sliding, or openable aluminum frames. Keep mosquitoes and insects out while letting fresh air in.

Pigeon & Bird Nets

UV-stabilized polyethylene bird netting to prevent pigeons, crows, and sparrows from entering balconies, ducts, and AC units. 100% bird proofing without harming birds.

Anti-Cat Nets

Extra-strong reinforced pet safety netting designed to prevent cats from jumping or falling off balconies and high windows while allowing them to enjoy the outdoors safely.

Cricket Practice & Sports Nets

Heavy-duty knotted and knotless sports practice nets for cricket turfs, schools, academies, and residential society sports grounds across Mumbai and Pune.
